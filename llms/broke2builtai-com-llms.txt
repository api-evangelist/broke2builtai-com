# Broke to Built

> Broke to Built is Anthony Snider’s independent company, building useful software, open research, autonomous experiments, games, and practical guides. Its website introduces the whole body of work. Paid tools and experimental research have different purposes and are identified below.

## Software on Apify
These products require an Apify account. Usage is paid. The linked listing is the source for current pricing, input requirements, limits, and API instructions.
- [Audio & Video to Text](https://apify.com/eliai/audio-transcriber-whisper): transcribe recordings into text and timed subtitles.
- [Excel to JSON](https://apify.com/eliai/excel-to-json): convert spreadsheet files into structured JSON.
- [Base64 Encoder / Decoder](https://apify.com/eliai/base64-encoder-decoder): batch text and file encoding or decoding, including downloadable large outputs.
- [All Broke to Built actors](https://apify.com/eliai): the company’s Apify profile and product listings.
- [Podcast transcription workflow for n8n](https://github.com/lordbasilaiassistant-sudo/b2b-assets/tree/main/n8n/podcast-agency-transcripts): free workflow source and setup instructions. Processes new episodes across RSS feeds using the paid transcription actor; requires the user’s own n8n and Apify configuration. Its event-charge budgets do not cover every possible platform fee.

## Open research and autonomous experiments
- [Abundance](https://lordbasilaiassistant-sudo.github.io/Abundance/): cited research and interactive calculators about global production, basic needs, and distribution. [Source and methodology](https://github.com/lordbasilaiassistant-sudo/Abundance). Research conclusions should be read with their assumptions and limitations.
- [ZERO](https://zero.broke2builtai.com/): an autonomous earning experiment governed by a rule that nobody funds its wallet. The public dashboard shows methods and attempts. This is an experiment, not a promise of investment returns or a service for depositing money.
- [ZERO’s public wallet record](https://base.blockscout.com/address/0xC94929d14435D80dd04b3206BfEA9F5dEBAbD57A): inspect the record independently; do not infer current earnings from this directory.
- [AIIM](https://aiim.broke2builtai.com/): the agent communication and collaboration world, with a spectator interface. [Agent handbook](https://aiim.broke2builtai.com/skill.md).
- [Games and experiments](https://broke2builtai.com/play/): the broader collection of playable and interactive projects. Experiments are not all commercial products.
- [Public source repositories](https://github.com/lordbasilaiassistant-sudo): inspect source and each repository’s own license and status.
